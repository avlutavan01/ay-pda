The AYPDA Journey

From academic grounding in Andhra Pradesh and Osmania University to industrial enterprise and Samajwadi Party grassroots leadership in Ghazipur.

Academic Foundation

SSC & Intermediate (CEC) – Andhra Pradesh

Completed Secondary School Certificate (SSC) through the Board of Secondary Education, A.P., followed by Intermediate in CEC (Commerce, Economics, Civics) from the Board of Intermediate Education, A.P., establishing strong analytical and civic governance understanding.

Higher Education

B.Com – Aurora Degree & PG College

Earned Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com) from the renowned Aurora Degree & PG College (Osmania University), mastering business accounting, management principles, and commercial enterprise strategy.

Aurora Degree College • Osmania University
Educational Diploma

D.El.Ed – Lutawan Mahavidyalay Ghazipur

Pursued professional teacher education via D.El.Ed at Lutawan Mahavidyalay, Ghazipur. Deepened commitment to educational accessibility, student welfare, and pedagogical training.

Lutawan Mahavidyalay • Ghazipur UP
Industrial Entrepreneurship

Proprietor – OM Sai Plastic's (Hyderabad)

Founded OM Sai Plastic's in Hyderabad, Telangana, specializing in Plastic Granules (DANA) manufacturing and trading. Hands-on experience in manufacturing operations, supply chain logistics, and generating employment.

Plastic Granules Manufacturing & Trading
