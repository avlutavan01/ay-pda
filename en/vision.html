Vision & PDA Mission

Empowering the marginalized through constitutional equality, educational support, industrial jobs, and Samajwadi Party grassroots leadership.

The Samajwadi Oath

"PDA is the soul of democratic justice. Our mission is to ensure that every youth in Ghazipur and across Uttar Pradesh gets dignity, education, and employment."

— Abhishek Yadav PDA (Admin, PDA Swabhimaan Portal • Jangipur 376 Ghazipur)

Core Pillars

Foundations of Democratic Progress

Concrete commitments addressing the real issues of students, farmers, youth, and workers in Jangipur 376, Ghazipur.

1. PDA Social Justice & Equity

Upholding the constitutional rights of Pichhda, Dalit, and Alpsankhyak communities. Guaranteeing fair representation in administrative opportunities and safeguarding social dignity.

  • Transparent citizen registration via PDA Swabhimaan Portal
  • Eradicating social discrimination and defending civil liberties

2. Educational Access & Teacher Welfare

Leveraging insights from D.El.Ed (Lutawan Mahavidyalay) and B.Com (Aurora College) to champion quality teacher training, primary school infrastructure, and affordable higher education for rural scholars.

  • Modern science labs and digital libraries in degree colleges
  • Scholarship tracking and student grievance redressing

3. Industrial Skilling & MSME Growth

Applying proven industrial experience from OM Sai Plastic's (Hyderabad) to advocate for manufacturing clusters, vocational skill centers, and self-employment funding for young entrepreneurs in Ghazipur.

  • Promoting MSME small-scale plastic & agro industries
  • Technical apprenticeships for rural college graduates

4. Jangipur 376 Infrastructure & Health

Supporting Hon'ble MLA Shri Virendra Kumar Yadav in expanding rural road connectivity, reliable electricity, clean drinking water, and upgrading primary health centers across Jangipur constituency.

  • Regular village listening camps for civic repairs
  • Upgrading local medical facilities and ambulances

5. Transparent Digital Democracy

As Social Media Incharge, utilizing social networks and portals to dismantle bureaucratic delays, publish factual updates, and provide citizens a direct line to their elected representatives.

  • Fast response to constituent feedback online
  • Countering fake propaganda with verified facts
