Political & Personal Profile

Leading the PDA Revolution with Dedication & Vision

Abhishek Yadav PDA is an energetic youth political leader, digital communicator, and entrepreneur serving the Samajwadi Party in Ghazipur, Uttar Pradesh. Currently designated as the Admin of the PDA Swabhimaan Portal for Jangipur 376 Assembly Constituency and the Social Media Incharge (SMI) for Hon'ble MLA Shri Virendra Kumar Yadav, Abhishek spearheads grassroots awareness campaigns that bring Samajwadi Party policies directly to the youth and rural families.

Combining academic rigor with real-world enterprise, Abhishek completed his D.El.Ed from Lutawan Mahavidyalay, Ghazipur, and graduated with a B.Com from Aurora Degree & PG College (Osmania University), following his foundational schooling through the Board of Intermediate Education (CEC) and Board of Secondary Education (SSC), Andhra Pradesh.

Alongside his committed political service, Abhishek is the founder and Proprietor of OM Sai Plastic's in Hyderabad, Telangana, a leading firm in Plastic Granules (DANA) manufacturing and trading, reflecting his deep understanding of industrial manufacturing, youth skilling, and job creation.
